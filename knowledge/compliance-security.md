# Compliance and Data Security in Mortgage Lending

> **DISCLAIMER**: This content is for educational purposes only. Consult compliance professionals and legal counsel for specific guidance.

## Key Compliance Themes

### Fair Lending
- **ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act)**: Prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, receipt of public assistance
- **Fair Housing Act**: Prohibits discrimination in housing-related transactions
- **Redlining**: Illegal practice of denying services to certain geographic areas
- **Disparate Impact**: Policies that appear neutral but disproportionately affect protected classes
- **Disparate Treatment**: Intentional discrimination

### TRID (TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure)
- Loan Estimate (LE): Provided within 3 business days of application
- Closing Disclosure (CD): Provided at least 3 business days before closing
- Tolerance rules for fee changes
- Changed circumstances documentation

### ATR/QM (Ability to Repay / Qualified Mortgage)
- Lenders must make reasonable, good-faith determination of ability to repay
- QM loans have certain protections (safe harbor or rebuttable presumption)
- QM criteria: debt-to-income, points and fees limits, loan term limits
- General QM rule revised in 2021 (price-based approach)

### RESPA (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act)
- Prohibits kickbacks and referral fees
- Affiliated business arrangement (AfBA) disclosures
- Servicing transfer notices
- Escrow account requirements

## Data Security

### SOC 2 (Service Organization Control 2)
- Framework for managing customer data
- Trust Service Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy
- Type 1: Point-in-time assessment
- Type 2: Assessment over a period (typically 6-12 months)
- Required by many enterprise clients and investors

### Key Security Controls
- **Encryption**: Data at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+, AES-256)
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access, MFA, least privilege
- **Audit Logging**: Track all data access and changes
- **Vulnerability Management**: Regular scanning and patching
- **Incident Response**: Documented procedures for breaches
- **Vendor Management**: Assess third-party security

### Privacy Regulations
- **GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act)**: Privacy notices, safeguards rule
- **CCPA/CPRA (California)**: Consumer privacy rights
- **State breach notification laws**: Vary by state

## AI Governance in Lending

### Regulatory Guidance
- CFPB has issued guidance on AI/ML in credit decisions
- Fair lending considerations for automated underwriting
- Adverse action notice requirements

### Key Principles
- **Explainability**: Ability to explain model decisions
- **Fairness Testing**: Regular bias testing across protected classes
- **Human Oversight**: Human review of significant decisions
- **Model Risk Management**: OCC SR 11-7 / Fed SR 11-7 guidance
- **Documentation**: Model development, validation, monitoring records

### Risks to Manage
- Training data bias
- Proxy discrimination
- Model drift over time
- Opacity of complex models
- Regulatory scrutiny of AI decisioning

## Examination and Audit

### Regulatory Examinations
- Federal: OCC, FDIC, Fed, NCUA, CFPB
- State: Department of Financial Services/Institutions
- Focus areas: fair lending, BSA/AML, consumer complaints, controls

### Internal Audit
- Independent assessment of controls
- Testing of key processes
- Remediation tracking
- Board/committee reporting

### Quality Control (QC)
- Pre-funding QC: Review before loan closes
- Post-closing QC: Sample review after closing
- Identify defects and trends
- Report to management and investors
