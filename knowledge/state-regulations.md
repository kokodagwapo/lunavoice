# 50-State Lending Regulation Reference

> **DISCLAIMER**: This is educational guidance only. State laws change frequently. Fee schedules, thresholds, and specific requirements must be verified with each state's regulator and legal counsel before operating. This is NOT legal or financial advice.

---

## Alabama (AL)
- **Regulator**: Alabama State Banking Department
- **License Types**: Mortgage Lender (ML), Mortgage Broker (MB), Mortgage Loan Originator (MLO)
- **Notable Rules**: State usury laws apply; high-cost loan triggers at 5% above prime for first liens
- **NMLS/CE**: Standard NMLS requirements; 8 hours annual CE for MLOs
- **Verify**: https://banking.alabama.gov

## Alaska (AK)
- **Regulator**: Alaska Division of Banking and Securities
- **License Types**: Mortgage Lender/Broker License
- **Notable Rules**: No state-specific high-cost loan law beyond federal; remote/exempt nature of many communities
- **NMLS/CE**: Standard NMLS; 8 hours annual CE
- **Verify**: https://www.commerce.alaska.gov/web/dbs

## Arizona (AZ)
- **Regulator**: Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions (DIFI)
- **License Types**: Mortgage Banker (MB), Mortgage Broker (BR), Commercial Mortgage Banker (CMB)
- **Notable Rules**: Anti-deficiency statutes for purchase money loans; specific advertising disclosure requirements
- **NMLS/CE**: 8 hours annual CE; fingerprinting required
- **Verify**: https://difi.az.gov

## Arkansas (AR)
- **Regulator**: Arkansas Securities Department
- **License Types**: Mortgage Banker, Mortgage Broker, MLO
- **Notable Rules**: State usury ceiling; high-cost threshold at 5% above prime for first liens
- **NMLS/CE**: Standard 8 hours CE
- **Verify**: https://securities.arkansas.gov

## California (CA)
- **Regulator**: Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI)
- **License Types**: California Financing Law (CFL), California Residential Mortgage Lending Act (CRMLA)
- **Notable Rules**: 
  - Comprehensive state-level consumer protection (CCPA/CPRA privacy)
  - Covered loan thresholds stricter than federal
  - Specific disclosure requirements for adjustable rates
  - Homeowner Bill of Rights for foreclosure protections
- **NMLS/CE**: 8 hours annual CE + 2 hours CA-specific
- **Verify**: https://dfpi.ca.gov

## Common Themes Across States

### High-Cost/Covered Loan Triggers
Most states have thresholds that may be stricter than federal HOEPA:
- APR exceeding prime + X% (typically 5-8%)
- Points and fees exceeding X% of loan amount
- Additional restrictions when triggered (prepay limits, required counseling)

### Foreclosure Procedures
- **Judicial** (court process required): FL, IL, NJ, NY, OH, PA, etc.
- **Non-Judicial** (trustee sale): AZ, CA, CO, GA, TX, WA, etc.
- **Hybrid/Either**: Some states allow both methods

### State Privacy Laws (Beyond Federal)
- California (CCPA/CPRA) — comprehensive
- Colorado (CPA) — comprehensive  
- Connecticut (CTDPA) — comprehensive
- Virginia (VCDPA) — comprehensive
- Utah (UCPA) — comprehensive
- More states considering similar legislation

### Multi-State Compliance
- Use NMLS for centralized licensing
- Maintain state-by-state disclosure matrices
- Subscribe to regulatory update services
- Consult with counsel in each state of operation
